# DuneCity 1.0.872

## QuantBot

### Campaign support

- **The support AI on your side attacks again when it reaches the unit limit.** It used to wait until its army was worth as much as the enemy's, which it could never reach once it had 25 units. It sat at home with its credits for the rest of the mission. It now attacks with the army it has.
- **The support AI upgrades its Construction Yard after its first Light Factory.** Since 1.0.870 it laid no concrete, so nothing asked for the upgrade and it often never happened. Your shared yard now gets the upgrade early, so concrete slabs and Rocket Turrets become available.
- On 528 test games over all 22 missions of the three houses, the support AI won 412 instead of 381 and ran out of time in 41 instead of 77.

### Skirmish and custom games

- **QuantBot now keeps its base powered when that pays for itself.** A power shortage costs repairs and refinery income, since a damaged refinery pays less for each load. QuantBot now buys the windtraps that close the shortage once those costs repay them within 25 game minutes. It builds them on concrete, and only when its Construction Yard has nothing else to build. On 338 test games on 13 maps it placed better (+0.06 placement score), and in All against Atreides it won all 36 games, 1.1 minutes faster.
- Campaign enemies and DuneCity city games are unchanged.

### Tested and not adopted

These exist as switches in the QuantBot config and are off: an earlier yard upgrade for anti-air defence, extra windtraps or late concrete for the campaign support AI, and a later yard upgrade or a bare-rock opening for skirmish QuantBots. None changed results measurably, and delaying the skirmish yard upgrade made it play worse.

## Tests

- Almost every automated test now also runs on Linux, so release checks no longer wait for one particular Mac.

## Known issues

- The Campaign AI and campaign helper known issues listed for 1.0.870 still apply.

## Compatibility

- Multiplayer uses **protocol 127**. All players need 1.0.872; it cannot play with 1.0.871.
- The save format is unchanged at **9886**.
- Android has a separate version and publication process.
