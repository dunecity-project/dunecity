# DuneCity 1.0.870

## Campaign AI opponent

- Fixed: the **Campaign AI** opponent was far too hard in 1.0.869, and unwinnable from level 6. It woke at the first moment of every mission and sent its whole starting army at your base within about two game minutes. As in the original Dune II, an enemy house now stays idle until one of your units has seen one of its ground units.
- It no longer forms mission attack teams. The original game never creates them in the campaign, so its attacks are newly built units and reinforcements arriving one or two at a time, not a massed group.
- Each Campaign AI house keeps the unit limit its mission gives it (15 to 25 units), as in the original. If you set a specific maximum number of units yourself, your number still applies.
- Measured in 264 Full campaign games with an idle human and a Hard QuantBot helper standing in for the player: levels 6 to 9 were won 51 of 54 times, against 0 of 54 in 1.0.869 and 49 of 54 in 1.0.867. Levels 1 to 5 were won 78 of 78 times. Every one of the 66 missions was won at least once. The first enemy unit reaches the base around minute 11 on those levels.
- A helper bot is a stand-in for a person. This has not yet been confirmed by people playing the missions.
- QuantBot opponents are a separate choice and are not changed by this. Games saved before 1.0.869 keep the earlier Campaign AI behaviour when loaded.

## QuantBot campaign helper

These apply to the QuantBot that plays alongside you in the classic campaign. Enemy QuantBots, skirmish, multiplayer and city campaigns are unchanged.

- **It holds its ground.** Troops that are not out on an attack no longer chase a target, or answer a contact, more than 14 tiles from the nearest building you own. They return to the base instead of being drawn out and lost one by one.
- **It builds a bigger economy first.** With a Starport available it builds six refineries before the Starport and grows to eight once the Repair Yard is up, where the map has the spice for it, and keeps one carryall for every two harvesters.
- **It lays concrete only under factories.** A building on bare rock starts at half health, and a factory at half health builds at half speed, so Light and Heavy Factories, the High-Tech Factory, WOR and Barracks still get concrete. Everything else goes straight onto rock, which is what pays for the extra refineries.
- **It builds its first Heavy Factory later.** With a Starport it builds the Repair Yard first and buys its early tanks from the Starport while they are at or below list price.
- Measured in Full campaign games with an idle human against Hard QuantBot opponents on missions 20 to 22, 216 games for each helper level on the same seeds: a Brutal helper won 129, up from 74 with the 1.0.869 helper. A Hard helper won 121, up from 95. A Medium helper won 81, up from 41. An Easy helper won 15, up from 3.
- On all 66 missions with four seeds each, a Brutal helper against Hard opponents won 240 of 264 games, up from 223.
- Against the Campaign AI opponent on all 66 missions with two seeds each, helpers of every level won 124 or 125 of 132 games.
- The gain comes from holding ground. The bigger build order on its own won 127 of 216 on one set of seeds and 116 against 115 on a second set, so it is not shown to add strength. It is included because it matches how the missions are usually played.

## Controls

- **Options > Hotkeys** is a new tab where each of the 25 gameplay commands can be given a key. Change, Clear and Defaults edit the list, Apply saves it, and Back discards unsaved changes.
- The default command keys are now single keys that stay clear of W, A, S and D: Q Attack, E Stop, F Guard, G Area Guard, Z Patrol, X Hold, V Move, R Repair, T Carryall, C Capture, B Place and Y for the next Construction Yard.
- Key bindings you have already customised are kept. Choose Defaults and then Apply to switch such a profile to the new keys.

## Known issues

- Late missions against Hard QuantBot opponents are still lost about four times in ten with a Brutal helper and an idle human. The weakest are Atreides mission 20 (4 of 24 won) and Harkonnen mission 21 (6 of 24).
- Against Brutal QuantBot opponents the helper lost all 216 late-mission test games, with or without the delayed Heavy Factory. This release does not change that.
- On missions 1 to 16 against the Campaign AI the helper changes made no measurable difference (92 of 96 games won at every helper level, against 91 to 94 before).

## Compatibility

- Multiplayer uses **protocol 126**: all players need 1.0.870.
- The save format is unchanged at **9886**.
- To restore the 1.0.869 helper, set `CampaignHelperDefenceLeash=0`, `CampaignHelperNoConcrete=0`, `CampaignHelperFactoryConcrete=0`, `CampaignHelperOpeningRefineries=0`, `CampaignHelperRefineryGoal=0`, `CampaignHelperCarryallRatio=0` and `CampaignHelperFactoryLast=0` in the QuantBot config.
- Android has a separate version and publication process.
