# DuneCity 1.0.874

## Fixes

- **Units a carryall delivers no longer chase enemies across the map.** With manual carryall drops on, a carryall flies new units to their factory's destination, and since 1.0.812 it also lifts units that are stuck. It used to set every unit it dropped to Area Guard, so they chased anything within ten tiles, including sandworms. Your units now land in the mode they had: Guard for a new unit, which is how a unit that drove there arrives. A unit you set to Hold or Ambush keeps that mode. Units on an attack-move or patrol keep fighting on their way, as before.
- AI units, saboteurs and harvesters are dropped as before.

## Compatibility

- Multiplayer uses **protocol 129**. All players need 1.0.874; it cannot play with 1.0.873.
- The save format is unchanged at **9886**.
- Android has a separate version and publication process.
