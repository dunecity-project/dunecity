# DuneCity 1.0.876

## New: an Easiest difficulty, and it is the default

- **QuantBot Easiest** is a new enemy difficulty below Easy. It plays like Easy with a smaller army, half the harvesters and no attack in the first ten minutes of a skirmish or custom game; as a campaign enemy it only rebuilds the army it starts with and sends smaller, later attack waves.
- **Easiest is now the default enemy** in the Campaign, the DuneCity Campaign, Skirmish and the AI seats of a Custom Game. Choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Brutal for more of a fight. Random Skirmish and Civilization Wars keep the enemy you chose last.
- **Restart on New Difficulty** in the in-game menu (single-player games): pick any difficulty from a list and the game starts again with every enemy on it, easier or harder. In a campaign the list also offers the original Campaign AI, and later missions carry on at the difficulty you picked. Your AI partner and allied AI stay as they are.

## Campaign enemies

- **Easy** enemies rebuild to one and a half times their starting army instead of twice.
- **Medium** enemies now raid with ornithopters, as Hard and Brutal do. Harvesters are still never an aircraft target.
- **Hard and Brutal** enemies add a spice silo when their storage is nearly full, as Medium already did.

## DuneCity

- **Parks**: a 1x1 park for 10 credits from the Construction Yard raises the land value of the homes and shops around it.
- **A Palace is one residential zone** now, without its commercial half; it still raises land value within eight tiles.
- The first-launch tip about the Dune City mod now explains where mods are chosen (the Mod list when starting a Campaign, Custom Game or Random Skirmish) instead of switching the mod for you.

## AI

- In DuneCity skirmish games the QuantBot runs a stronger city economy: one reactor instead of rows of Wind Traps, Palace and Stadium districts placed for land value, and it finds room for a Palace in a built-up city.

## Statistics

- Each installation now sends a random anonymous id with its own player's game statistics, so the public usage page can count players who all use the default name. It holds nothing about your device, account, name or network, and resetting the settings resets it.

## Compatibility

- Multiplayer uses **protocol 132**. All players need 1.0.876; it cannot play with 1.0.875.
- The save format is now **9888**. 1.0.876 opens older saves; 1.0.875 cannot open saves from 1.0.876.
- Android has a separate version and publication process.
