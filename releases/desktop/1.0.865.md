# DuneCity 1.0.865

## Matches and maps

- Civilization Wars adds generated team battlefields, starting cities and a dedicated rules mod.
- Campaign missions are available as named maps in Custom Game and the map editor.
- Export a match's starting map or current state as a new custom map. Use Save Game to continue the original match.
- Random Skirmish gains terrain-mix controls and fairer contiguous building rock around starting positions.

## City building and research

- Each house can set its own city tax rate. City land value, power status and vacant lots have clearer rules and overlays.
- Shared Research Labs provide technology progression, with fixed research prices and a shared Research Pace setting. Trooper anti-air support is available through its match rule.
- Optional Technology Installments spreads technology payments over the study or upgrade. It remains off by default.
- Change your house's QuantBot partner between None, Build, Fight and Both during a match.
- Updated scoped QuantBot policies cover campaign recovery, opening economy, unit counters, city capital pacing and combat decisions. Unpromoted experimental policies remain opt-in.

## Mouse and trackpad controls

- Mouse-wheel zoom stays anchored at the pointer; middle-button dragging pans the map.
- Mac two-finger scrolling pans without holding a button, and pinch/spread zooms at the pointer. Vertical panning is reversed to match the horizontal direction convention; physical mouse-wheel zoom is unchanged.

## Multiplayer and stability

- Incompatible multiplayer listings are filtered, Join clicks during refresh are handled, and duplicate-name errors are clearer.
- Retired peer signaling is discarded, with stricter handling of stale connection completions.
- Windows online lobbies and nested setup screens avoid large stack allocations.
- A* search, terrain scans and retained simulation data use less repeated work and memory.

## Linux startup and rendering

- Linux startup can recover with a software renderer when acceleration cannot initialize, while still requiring target-texture support and respecting explicit SDL driver preferences.
- Linux packages include the graphics and input dependencies required for desktop OpenGL/EGL and IBus, with package-level video diagnostics.
- Terrain remains visible beneath transparent Police Station, Nuclear Plant, Stadium and Airport corners. Existing transparent Research Lab and Tornie artwork retain their terrain backing; opaque vanilla building culling is unchanged.

## Compatibility

Multiplayer uses **protocol 122**: all players must use a compatible 1.0.865 build, not 1.0.864 (protocol 109). New saves use **save format 9884**. Android has a separate version and publication process.
