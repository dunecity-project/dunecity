# Dune City 1.0.829

Ornithopters now hunt enemy combat units on **Hard and Brutal**, prioritising nearby launchers when their wing can engage. **Easy and Medium** defend their own bases and harvesters and raid enemy bases only when the approach and target are clear of observed anti-air. Autonomous ornithopters never hunt harvesters.

- Aircraft target planning shares bounded caches rather than scanning the world for every aircraft. Launchers regroup with attacking armies and recover from stalled wave orders. QuantBot reserves a mobile anti-air share when hostile aircraft are present and can build Troopers and their required infantry technology.
- Killing a high-performing enemy unit type earns a larger combat-value bonus. The bonus follows the victim type's measured performance, with bounded rewards and a fallback while evidence is scarce.
- The new **Stats** menu shows active units, kills, deaths and damage dealt, plus units produced, spice harvested, tax collected and population. QuantBot performance appears as **xN.N**, alongside each type's target army-value percentage. Observers can browse every house's Stats and Budget, with the house name visible.
- **1920 × 1080** and other supported HD render choices now appear on Retina displays. The chosen render resolution remains saved while the window fits the desktop without changing its aspect ratio. Native interface sizing remains available.
- Direct multiplayer avoids flooding a waiting peer with identical command windows, preserving immediate new-order delivery and periodic retries.
- Includes airport ambient aircraft and three additional community maps: Reacciona rapido forro, Alto mapa HD and La guerra dei ricchi.

Validation covered 14 bounded AI games across Vanilla, Dune2R, Dune City, campaign and all four difficulties, with three matched 1.0.820 baseline runs. One city-layout case was excluded from city-simulation coverage because it used Vanilla. No crashes or ornithopter harvester targets were recorded. Hard city combat shifts strongly toward aircraft; tested launcher and rocket-turret counters, army-wave progress and city economies remained viable. These runs establish the tested scenarios, not universal player balance.

Existing saves remain loadable. Multiplayer protocol is **67**: all peers must update together. Saves, settings and user maps remain in the separate user folder.
