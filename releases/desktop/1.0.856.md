# DuneCity 1.0.856

- The Dune2 DuneCity skin now shows in every mode. In Custom Game, a row left on
  Random gets the skin chosen for it. Skirmish and Random Skirmish each have a skin
  choice, SimCity or Dune2, that applies to every house. The choice is remembered
  together with the campaign's DuneCity skin.
- DuneCity saves made before the 1.0.854/1.0.855 artwork update load again as the
  shipped Dune City mod.
- Spectated games end when they are decided and name the winner. Online spectators
  watch to the real end even after the host leaves.
- Sandworms and crime troopers now belong to a hidden Natives house. They never
  count toward unit limits, victory or statistics, and summoned reinforcement worms
  spare the house that called them.
- The city airplane flies straight legs on 16 headings. When it is shot down it
  detonates like a Death Hand.
- AI: the AI (QuantBot) places its Palace and House of IX only on sites where they
  fit, so a construction yard is no longer left waiting on an unplaceable building.
- Twelve community maps and three reviewed community mod presets are bundled:
  deo-t, TornUge and "new - modded".
- Faster eight-house games, with lower build-pass and city-growth spikes. The idle
  main menu no longer redraws every button each frame. Desert-skin menus keep clear
  of the background's corner crests.
- Includes Vuk's 1.0.853–1.0.855 artwork and payload work.

Network protocol 103, save version 9874. Older saves load. Multiplayer peers need
the same version.
