# Dune City 1.0.810

The game-speed controls now reach 3 ms and 2 ms per simulation cycle, twice the previous 4 ms maximum. The default stays at 16 ms and the slow end at 32 ms; existing saved speed choices are kept. A controlled 60 Hz measurement reached about 333 and 500 cycles per second at the new settings, but slow rendering, heavy simulation or high latency can hold a match below its configured rate. This is not a frame-rate improvement for large CPU-bound armies.

- Observer checkpoint recovery replaces an open game menu with working join-progress controls and closes it before the match is rebuilt.
- Custom Hard and Brutal QuantBot sends every available ground combat unit to Hunt in one pass, and a real attack on any owned building — including a remote R/C/I colony — scrambles the ground army to the invasion. Hunting launchers keep their close-range escape and resume firing; reserves assemble at a protected rally outside the colony footprint. Human orders, repair, workers and aircraft keep their existing authority.
- Bases grow overlapping rocket batteries towards the observed enemy approach. In DuneCity Custom Hard/Brutal games, a battery with no free site can reclaim an owned residential, commercial or industrial lot on the enemy-facing side. Free ground always wins, and local lot minimums, hospitals, churches, roads, factory exits and corridors are preserved.
- Crowded armies waste less pathfinding work and keep valid cached routes. A ground unit stalled on one tile for 30 seconds with an outstanding movement order can request an owned free carryall.
- Carryall pickup no longer loses its passenger, and bookings are released on both sides. Units orphaned by that fault in older saves return to the map, so hidden units can no longer keep a match from ending.
- Police Stations no longer claim "Unit limit reached" below the game's own unit limit. They use the house's existing Trike and Trooper admission rules, and Brutal's separate military-value gate now reads "Army target reached".
- Custom Game offers a per-house spice income multiplier of 1x to 5x for players and bots, explained above the roster: it multiplies credits from harvester deliveries, with 1x the original rate. The "Original" house-colour selector and its bonus-palette checkbox were removed; saved colour choices remain intact.

Existing saves remain loadable, and saves written before these changes default to a 1x spice rate. Save format is 9852. Multiplayer protocol is 56, which excludes peers on older builds: all players must update together before playing online. Saves, settings and user mods remain in the separate user folder.
