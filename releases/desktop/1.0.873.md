# DuneCity 1.0.873

## Reworked (Remastered) campaign

- **The Campaign screen has a new Maps choice: Original or Reworked.** Reworked plays harder, larger versions of the campaign missions, based on Dmitri Fatkin's reworked missions for Dune Legacy. Every menu of a reworked campaign and the game screen show a REMASTERED badge.
- **Atreides, Harkonnen and Ordos each have all 22 missions.** Fatkin reworked Atreides 1-11, 21 and 22, Harkonnen 1-3 and Ordos 1. DuneCity built the rest from the original missions in his style. With Reworked selected, the house list shows only these three houses.
- **What changes in a reworked mission.** The map, your start and the original enemy base stay the same. From mission 5 a second enemy house has its own base. Every enemy has harvesters, there are more defenders and spice fields, more AI attack teams and reinforcement waves, and you start with more credits.
- **Everything else works as in the normal campaign.** Briefings, region map, debriefing and statistics, Full campaign or Single mission, the AI partner, the enemy AI (QuantBot at any difficulty or the Campaign AI), saves and co-op all work as before. Co-op games show the badge in the game screen only.
- **Tested at every difficulty.** All 66 missions were played against their originals on the same seeds with QuantBot Easy, Medium, Hard and Brutal enemies and the Campaign AI, with an AI player in your seat. At Easy, Medium and Hard and against the Campaign AI they play about like the originals and take a little longer. The final missions were eased after testing. Enemy reinforcements in missions 20-22 now arrive from minute 10, and Fatkin's Atreides 21 and 22 follow the same timing.

## Fixes

- **A unit placed on a spice bloom when a map starts no longer becomes invisible.** It used to set the bloom off, die and come back as a unit nobody could see or attack, so its house could never be defeated and the mission never ended. It now waits on the bloom, which goes off when another unit drives onto it. No bundled map puts a unit on a bloom; custom maps could.

## Known issues

- **Reworked missions are much harder against Brutal QuantBot enemies** than the originals. In testing, an AI player with Brutal support won 12 of 66 reworked missions against Brutal enemies, against 27 of 66 originals. Brutal attack waves have no credit limit, so the reworked enemies' larger economy becomes larger waves.
- With Easy QuantBot support, some reworked level 7 and 8 missions take longer than 80 minutes to win.
- Fremen, Mercenary and Sardaukar have no complete reworked campaign. Reworked is not offered with the Tornie editions, which bring their own campaigns.
- The Campaign AI and campaign helper known issues listed for 1.0.870 still apply.

## Compatibility

- Multiplayer uses **protocol 128**. All players need 1.0.873; it cannot play with 1.0.872.
- The save format is unchanged at **9886**.
- Android has a separate version and publication process.
