# DuneCity 1.0.842

- **Unit orders:** attack-move (A), patrol (Q), Hold position, Shift-queued
  waypoints and repeat production.
- **Formations:** select two or more ground units and choose Box, Pincer,
  Column, Staggered or Echelon in the unit panel (Ctrl+F on/off,
  Ctrl+Shift+F cycles). The units form up where they stand, then move,
  attack-move and patrol as one group at one speed. Siege tanks lead,
  harvesters stay out, an MCV takes the centre, and nothing stands in a
  sonic tank's line of fire.
- **Plan building sites:** queue buildings and place them in advance;
  they are built on the plan when ready, spread over all Construction
  Yards. This is a game rule (Game Rules: "Plan building sites when
  ordering").
- **Random Skirmish:** generated maps with natural terrain, clustered
  bases, zoned cities and nuclear power. Choose your start, an AI partner,
  0-6 allies and 1-7 enemies, rock up to 100%, any installed mod and the
  game rules. Random army and city sizes are the same for every house.
- **Multiplayer random maps:** hosts choose "Random map..." in the map
  chooser, set the size, terrain and what every house starts with, then
  players join and set houses and teams in the normal lobby.
- **Sidebar:** the build list is a two-column grid, so a full Construction
  Yard fits without scrolling.
- **QuantBot:** escorts a moving MCV in a ring. Its attack waves keep the
  usual behaviour: no formation won more in 1,980 test games.
- Saves from 1.0.833 still load. Multiplayer needs every player on
  1.0.842.
