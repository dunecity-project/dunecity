# DuneCity 1.0.850

- **Faster big games:** large maps with many houses run about twice as
  fast. An 8-house 384×384 game went from about 19 to 42 fps on a MacBook
  Air in Low Power Mode. When the computer can't keep up, single-player
  games now slow down slightly instead of stuttering.
- **The AI attacks in free-for-alls:** QuantBot armies no longer sit in
  their bases when several houses play. A house now judges whether it is
  outnumbered against its strongest single enemy, gathers outside its own
  city, and a leading house goes after the weakest one. Games end with a
  winner far more often.
- **Difficulty levels:** Brutal > Hard > Medium > Easy now holds in long
  games. Medium attacks with its whole army, and Brutal builds a bigger
  economy using its higher harvester and army limits. No difficulty
  cheats on resources.
- **Unit mixes:** each house starts with a tuned unit mix (Atreides,
  Harkonnen, Ordos, Fremen) and a mix designed for each tech level, e.g.
  tank-heavy at tech 4. No unit type starts above 50% or below 2%, and the
  AI still adapts during the game.
- **Campaign:** the AI partner wins most late missions, and DuneCity
  missions that never finished now end. City tax credits count towards a
  spice quota.
- **Fixed:** missions in the DuneCity mod were won as soon as the city
  reached 500 people.
- **Fixed:** the Attack button or A key on a spice bloom now shoots the
  bloom from range instead of driving onto it.
- **Removed:** sharing maps to the online catalogue and browsing it.
  Hosting a game with your own mod still works.
- Saves from 1.0.842 still load. Multiplayer needs every player on
  1.0.850.
