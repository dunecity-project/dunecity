# DuneCity 1.0.867

## Campaign difficulty

- Easy, Medium and Hard campaign opponents now differ in clear steps: they keep at most 1, 2 and 4 harvesters, send attack waves worth 1,700, 2,500 and 3,500, and a Hard opponent waits one more minute after the map's first enemy reinforcement before its first attack. Easy opponents keep their smaller waves on early missions.
- Brutal campaign opponents are unchanged: full strength, no harvester ceiling and no opening delay.
- A QuantBot helper on your side follows its own difficulty. Easy, Medium and Hard helpers keep at most 4, 6 and 8 harvesters. Hard and Brutal helpers build a ground army before they grow their economy.
- Helpers hold their ground attacks until their army matches the strongest enemy alliance, instead of sending small waves one at a time.
- On spice-quota missions a helper that shares your house stops spending once half the quota is banked, or when the map is running short of spice, so the mission can be won on credits.
- Single mission and Single mission co-op use the same QuantBot rules as the full campaign.

## Performance

- Long matches with large armies and exhausted spice fields cost less AI time: army assembly space and harvester drop-off regions are worked out once instead of repeatedly.

## Names and notices

- The city graphics skin is labelled Classic, and the bundled two-player city map is now "2P - 192x192 - City".
- The About screen states that DuneCity is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts, Westwood Studios, the Herbert estate or any Dune licensee.

## Compatibility

Multiplayer uses **protocol 123**: all players need 1.0.867, not 1.0.865 (protocol 122). New saves use **save format 9885**; saves from earlier versions still load. Android has a separate version and publication process. Version 1.0.866 was tagged but never published.
