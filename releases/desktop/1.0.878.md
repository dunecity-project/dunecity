# DuneCity 1.0.878

## City airplanes

- **A city airplane that is shot down now crashes with one bomb's blast** on the tile where it falls, instead of a Death Hand impact. In test games this cut the buildings a crash destroyed from about 80 to about 10 a game, and a flight now destroys about 30% less for its cost.
- **Anti-air always fires at enemy planes.** Rocket Turrets, Launchers and Troopers no longer hold their fire when the plane is over their own base.

## Compatibility

- Multiplayer uses **protocol 134**. All players need 1.0.878; it cannot play with 1.0.877.
- The save format is unchanged (**9898**): 1.0.877 and 1.0.878 open each other's saves.
- Android has a separate version and publication process.
