# DuneCity 1.0.880

## City airplanes

- **Every Airport has its own free plane**, each on its own five-minute clock. A second Airport now means a second free plane.
- **Airports build bought planes.** Build airplane (1,500) is paid when ordered and takes 57.6 seconds at that Airport, with a progress bar in the Airport panel; one at a time per Airport, so more Airports build more planes at once.
- **An Airport built during the game launches its free plane straight away**, also in the first five minutes. Airports that stand when the game begins still charge for five minutes first.
- **A plane that is shot down explodes as three of its bombs** along its path, so a crash over a city takes out several buildings.
- **The computer player builds extra Airports** (up to 4) when their free planes and production pay back their price within 20 minutes, and up to 8 when it is rich and every Airport is busy.
- The new plane rules are on by default and can be switched off in the game rules (City Plane Free Per Airport, City Plane Build Time, City Plane Crash Bombs).

## Saboteurs

- **Saboteurs get credit for what they blow up.** They always destroyed their targets, but the Stats screen showed 0 kills and 0 damage and counted every detonation as a death. Kills and damage now show, and a saboteur that blew something up is no longer counted as lost.
- **A bigger blast**, and spectators see each saboteur with a ring in its owner's colour, a line to its target and a mark on the radar.

## AI

- **No fixed Trooper share.** The computer player's infantry is scored on how it performs, like every other unit type, instead of a fixed 10-18% quota.
- **The computer player may build right up to enemy buildings**, where the game's placement rules allow it.
- **The computer player keeps its Windtraps** unless its base is running out of room; it used to remove them once it had a Nuclear Plant.

## Fixes

- **The DuneCity Campaign no longer crashes when started at Mission 10** (All Against Atreides) as a full campaign.

## Compatibility

- Multiplayer uses **protocol 142**. All players need 1.0.880; it cannot play with 1.0.879.
- Save format **9899**: 1.0.880 opens older saves, but 1.0.879 cannot open saves made by 1.0.880.
- Android has a separate version and publication process.
