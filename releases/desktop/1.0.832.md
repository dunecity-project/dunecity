# Dune City 1.0.832

QuantBot armies now fight when their bases are attacked, instead of marching back and forth across the map. On Hard and Brutal, an attack on any building used to send the whole army after whichever enemy fired last, with orders that stopped units shooting on the way; with several colonies under attack at once, launchers and tanks kept turning around and hardly fired.

- The troops nearest the attacked building now answer it, enough of them to outweigh the attackers, and only from within a bounded distance that grows with the size of the attack.
- They travel in Hunt mode and attack any enemy they meet on the way, rather than ignoring it until they arrive. They are not sent elsewhere while they are on their way, fighting, or while enemies remain at that building.
- Units already carrying out another forced order, such as a launcher stepping back to firing range, are left to finish it.

On a reported 3waysplit save, Sardaukar launchers went from 7 shots to 296 over the same two minutes of play.

Existing saves remain loadable. Multiplayer protocol is **69**: all peers must update together. Saves, settings and user maps remain in the separate user folder.
