# DuneCity 1.0.868

## Campaign AI

- QuantBot helpers build a Repair Yard at every difficulty. In 1.0.867 a Medium helper that started a mission without one never added it, so its damaged tanks were not repaired. From tech level 5, every helper and every Medium, Hard and Brutal campaign opponent now plans at least one Repair Yard, with the Outpost and Light Factory it needs. Only Easy opponents stay without one unless the mission gives it to them.
- Campaign QuantBots no longer upgrade their Construction Yard straight away. They wait until they have power and spice income, their harvesting is set up, and they can pay for the upgrade and still keep a reserve of at least 2,000 credits.
- Measured in Single mission games on the original rules with an idle human: a Medium helper against Hard opponents won 16 of 36 games, up from 10. A Hard helper against Medium opponents was unchanged within the margin of the test (32 of 36 against 31). Mission 22 with a Medium helper stayed at 0 wins of 12: this release does not make that mission winnable without the player's own army.

## Compatibility

Multiplayer uses **protocol 124**: all players need 1.0.868, not 1.0.867 (protocol 123). The save format is unchanged at **9885**, and a Single mission save made with 1.0.867 on the original rules was loaded and continued in this version. Android has a separate version and publication process.
