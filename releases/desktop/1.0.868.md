# DuneCity 1.0.868

## QuantBot AI

- The campaign rule that denies a Repair Yard by difficulty now applies only to Easy opponents. In 1.0.867 a Medium helper that started a mission without a Repair Yard never added one, so its damaged tanks were not repaired. Helpers of every difficulty, and Medium and harder opponents, now build one. The usual conditions still apply: tech level, credits, a place to build, and in city games the technology the map allows.
- Campaign QuantBots no longer upgrade their Construction Yard straight away. They wait until they have income (spice, or taxes in a city game) and the power they are required to have, and can pay for the upgrade while keeping a reserve of at least 2,000 credits.
- Measured in Single mission games on the original rules, missions 14, 20 and 22, with an idle human: a Medium helper against Hard opponents won 16 of 36 games, up from 10. A Hard helper against Medium opponents was unchanged within the margin of the test (32 of 36 against 31). On mission 22 a Medium helper still lost all 12 tested games; no improvement is established there.
- Custom games on the original rules: a Medium QuantBot is no longer limited to the Repair Yards it starts with, or a single one if it starts with none. Like the other difficulties it now builds them as needed. This follows from the same rule change and was not measured in matched games.

## Compatibility

Multiplayer uses **protocol 124**: all players need 1.0.868, not 1.0.867 (protocol 123). The save format is unchanged at **9885**, and a Single mission save made with 1.0.867 on the original rules was loaded and continued in this version. Android has a separate version and publication process.
