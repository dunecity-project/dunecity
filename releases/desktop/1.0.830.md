# Dune City 1.0.830

QuantBot now spreads its bases across the map. Once its home rock is built out or already holds two construction yards, a new MCV drives to free rock elsewhere and founds a colony there, instead of stacking more yards on the same formation.

- Colonists travel with an escort. Each MCV heading for new rock waits on its home rock until its three nearest free vehicles have gathered, then drives out with them. Escorts guard the MCV on the way and turn on anything that damages it. A colonist under fire keeps driving instead of stopping in place.
- An MCV boxed in by buildings with only diagonal exits can now find a way out and deploy. Previously it could stand idle for the rest of the match and block further MCV purchases.
- Colonising no longer waits for three rocket turrets over every outlying yard. Each new colony still orders its own turret cover once its yard stands.
- Colonists lost on the way are remembered: two recent losses pause new colonists until they age out, so the AI does not feed MCVs into a contested corridor.
- QuantBot's internal organisation was restructured with no change to its decisions; seeded AI matches produce identical decision logs before and after.

Validation covered Sihaya-Ferryman Brutal-versus-Brutal matches on three seeds over 50 game minutes, with both houses founding one to three colonies and no MCV left idle at the end.

Existing saves remain loadable. Multiplayer protocol is **68**: all peers must update together. Saves, settings and user maps remain in the separate user folder.
