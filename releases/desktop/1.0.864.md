# DuneCity 1.0.864

## Building and planning

- With nothing selected, the sidebar shows buttons for your Construction Yard and each
  factory type. A button opens the least busy building of that type.
- The build list highlights the building you are placing or planning a site for.
- Shared Factory Queue (match rule, on by default, human houses only): factories of the
  same type share one queue, so an idle factory takes the next waiting order. The order
  already in production never moves. Starports, Chaos Factories and Yards keep their own
  queues.
- Roads can be planned like buildings: each planned tile is reserved, and the Yard lays
  it when its turn comes.

## Matches and maps

- Custom Game: a new **AI support** checkbox puts a Builder QuantBot in your house's
  second seat. Change its difficulty or role in that seat.
- Random Skirmish and Random Map: a new **Sandworms** setting (None to 8) places
  starting worms on open sand, away from every base. The rest of the map is unchanged.
- New community map: **8P - 384x384 - Colonist** (CC-BY-SA).

## Play Online

- **Create Random Skirmish** generates a map and opens the online player setup for it.
- Running games say **In progress**, and the button reads **Spectate**.
- A failed direct connection now says at which stage it failed, and logs the connection
  types tried (no addresses), so failures can be diagnosed. There is still no relay.

Network protocol 109 and save version 9879: play online with 1.0.864 players.
