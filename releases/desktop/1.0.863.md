# DuneCity 1.0.863

## Tornie 1.0.546 and five new Tornie-family mods

Everything new in Tornie_Panther's Dune Legacy Tornie up to 1.0.546 now plays in
Dune City. Choose the mod in the Mod list when you pick a campaign house or set up a
custom game. These changes apply only while one of the Tornie-family mods is active;
Vanilla, Dune City and Dune2R play exactly as before.

- New mods: **Jericho**, **Jericho Lite**, **Tornie Lite**, **ArcaKev** and
  **ArcaKev Lite**, alongside Tornie. Lite editions use six houses.
- Twelve playable factions in campaigns, custom games and the map editor, including
  Wildspade, Kleshmersh and Tharpique, with Tornie's 1.0.546 campaigns: two
  opponents in early missions, three in missions 20–21 and four in the finale, a
  permanent support enemy per campaign, a CHOAM-bought MCV for the Rebels opening and
  loaned aircraft for Wildspade.
- New units and buildings: Flame Quad, Gas Infantry, Flame Trooper, Elite
  Ornithopter, Raider Quad, Ranged Tank, Slow Tank with Slowpost, Tame Sandworm,
  Fast and Capacity Harvesters, the two-bay Doublefinery, five-Soldier and
  five-Trooper orders, and the Neutral Saboteur.
- Sell buildings for 50% of their cost (75% for an Easy allied house), upgrade the
  Rebels Windtrap, research Radar upgrades, play Chaos Mode, and let allies control
  units or build on allied ground when the lobby allows it.
- Captured factories keep the technology of the house that built them.
- Easy Mode for campaigns: start at mission 2, cheaper purchases and extra credits.
- Faction co-op campaigns: up to four allied houses with two players each, over the
  usual direct connection. The existing two-players-one-house co-op is unchanged.
- Lilac and white spice, two-colour spice sets per mod, a scrolling CHOAM list in the
  map editor, harvester and aircraft counts on the HUD, animal Mentats, faction
  frames and 73 offline achievements.
- Fixed: game data could be read by two loading threads at once during startup.

## Since 1.0.858

- Choose AI, difficulty and role (Builder, Fighter or Both) for every AI in every
  menu. Fighter commands units but never builds.
- Construction Yards take planned orders from one shared queue, planned plots keep
  retrying when units block them, and a finished plan that can never get build range
  is cancelled and refunded.
- A city year lasts 120 seconds, slowing budget payouts and zone growth (match rule,
  on by default). The budget window shows storage and warns when city tax is lost
  without a Refinery or Silo.
- In Dune City matches the Construction Yard stores 1000 credits.
- QuantBot saves for rocket-turret upgrades in cities, clears its opening MCV
  footprint, and still buys its first Refinery.
- Random maps have about a third less mountain.
- Sonic Tank and sandworm shimmer no longer draw black circles at in-between zoom
  steps.

Saves from earlier versions still load. Multiplayer players need 1.0.863 and the same
mod; this version cannot play online with 1.0.862 or older.
