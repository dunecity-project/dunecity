# DuneCity 1.0.875

## New: the DuneCity Campaign

- **Ten Atreides city missions**, from the main menu's new **DuneCity Campaign** item (it replaces Continue; Load Game still lists every save). The missions grow from settling a basin to holding a capital against every other house: reach a population, fill the treasury, keep crime down, raise land value, hold out, or destroy the enemy, and in Shield Wall Breach the wall opens at minute 12 and lets sandworms in.
- **Choose how you play** as in the Campaign: full campaign or a single mission, which mission to start from, the DuneCity skin, an AI partner and the enemy AI. The campaign uses the DuneCity mod and is offline for now.
- **Your Mentat briefs every mission** and then lists its goals beside Proceed, for example "Goal: 20,000 people, held for 1 minute. Or destroy every enemy house. Enemy attacks can begin at minute 2."
- **After each win the planet takes your colour**: Arrakis fades in, turns into the region map, and the regions this mission wins turn Atreides blue. After the tenth, all of Arrakis is yours.
- **An Objectives panel** at the top right of the map shows each goal's progress, the hold countdown and any time limit.
- **Low spice, room to build**: the economy runs on taxes rather than spice, and every map has rock for cities on both sides.
- **The enemy AI difficulty sets the challenge**: each mission's enemy strength and the time before its first attack depend on the difficulty you choose. A helper AI one level above the enemies wins most games; at the same level it loses most.

## AI

- In every DuneCity game, the QuantBot now places Palaces and Stadiums among its homes and shops to raise land value (and with it tax income), and buys a Palace for that once its city has a dozen homes and shops.
- In the DuneCity Campaign the difficulty tiers play differently: higher tiers decide faster and use more city skills, and a Hard or Brutal QuantBot with money for units and a busy factory builds another factory instead of zoning more city with all its construction yards.

## Compatibility

- Multiplayer uses **protocol 130**. All players need 1.0.875; it cannot play with 1.0.874.
- The save format is now **9887**. 1.0.875 opens older saves; 1.0.874 cannot open saves from 1.0.875.
- Android has a separate version and publication process.
