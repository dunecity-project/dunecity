# DuneCity 1.0.877

## New: city airplanes that bomb, and anti-air that shoots them down

- **Bombing runs.** A City Airplane now drops a stick of heavy bombs as it flies over enemy buildings and enemy ground units. One hit destroys a zone, Wind Trap, turret, Heavy Factory or Repair Yard; two destroy most other buildings. A bomb that just misses still damages enemy buildings within a tile and a half; your own and allied buildings take no splash. Bombs fall behind the plane and never hurt aircraft.
- **Buy planes at the Airport** for 1500 credits each, as fast as you can pay, up to ten in the air. Computer players buy them while their planes destroy as much per credit as their army does, or when they have 30,000 credits to spare.
- **A free plane** from each powered Airport every five minutes. Nobody bombs in the first five minutes of a game: an Airport that stands at the start, or is built in the opening, has its first plane ready at five minutes; one built later has a plane ready at once.
- **Rocket Turrets and Launchers shoot planes down**, including over their own city. Turrets reach a plane at 16 tiles and their rockets give up the chase beyond that. A plane takes five turret rockets.
- **A plane that is shot down still crashes** with a Death Hand blast. What the crash destroys now counts for the house that sent the plane, not for whoever shot it down.
- A computer player that is being bombed adds Rocket Turrets through its city.
- **The Airport is 4x4 tiles**, drawn from the full Micropolis airport. As in Micropolis it has no population of its own, lets commercial zones keep growing and pollutes twice as much as a fully built industrial zone. It no longer launches Ornithopters and has a shortcut on the side menu. An Airport in an older save, or on a map with no room, stays 3x3.
- Planes have their own icon. In the Stats window the unit table calls them City Airplane, and a computer player's Army allocation table has a row for the City Airplane and one for Troopers, each with its performance score and a percentage the note under the table explains.

## Units

- **Troopers** answer an Ornithopter with rockets as soon as it is in rocket range, without first turning to face it.
- **Saboteurs** go for the nearest enemy building or vehicle, blow up when they reach it and pick a new target if theirs is gone. Computer players no longer pull a hunting Saboteur into army orders. A Saboteur that is shot falls like a foot soldier instead of exploding, and its owner sees the target before the blast.

## Carryalls and repair

- **Manual Carryall Drops is on by default in Dune City**, including for players whose settings file stored it as off. Untick it once more if you turned it off on purpose.
- **Return units on / off** in the Repair Yard's side panel: a repaired unit is flown back to where it was picked up, or left beside the yard. One switch for all of your Repair Yards.
- On Hard and Brutal, in Custom games without the city simulation that start from a Construction Yard, the computer player flies a few spare units onto enemy harvesters that nothing is guarding.

## Custom Game

- **Tech level** can be chosen when setting up a Custom Game.

## Compatibility

- Multiplayer uses **protocol 133**. All players need 1.0.877; it cannot play with 1.0.876.
- The save format is now **9898**. 1.0.877 opens older saves; 1.0.876 cannot open saves from 1.0.877.
- Android has a separate version and publication process.
