# DuneCity campaign

Stefan, 2026-11-19: "build a dune city campaign ... maps should be about defeating enemies,
reaching a certain number of credits or growing to a certain population size, reducing crime and
increasing property values etc. as well as things like there is a breach in the shield wall ...
play test the scenarios so that the support ai can win against an enemy ai one level lower than
itself ... the maps should get harder so the win rate falls a bit even with the difficulty
difference ... aim for 10 maps, including a version of all against atreides which allows for city
building and has room for it for the enemy too."

Ten Atreides missions, `data/maps/singleplayer/DCITY_M01.INI` .. `DCITY_M10.INI`, generated by
`scripts/gen_city_campaign.py` from the city-scenario toolkit (`scripts/gen_city_scenarios.py`).
Play them from **Campaign → Maps: DuneCity** with the DuneCity mod active (city mode). Missions
follow each other in order; a lost mission is replayed. There is no region map.

## Mission goals: `[OBJECTIVES]`

An optional section of any scenario INI (`include/MissionObjectives.h`):

| Key | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| `Population=N` | displayed city population of the player's house (internal population x 20) at least N |
| `Credits=N` | the treasury the sidebar shows (`House::getCredits`) at least N |
| `MaxCrime=N` | average crime at the house's developed buildings at most N (0-255; Safe is below 64) |
| `MinLandValue=N` | average land value at least N (0-250; Middle Class from 80) |
| `MaxPollution=N` | average pollution at most N |
| `DefeatEnemies=1` | every enemy house must also be gone |
| `HoldSeconds=N` | every goal must hold together for N seconds (default 30) |

All listed goals must hold at the same time for `HoldSeconds`. Crime, land value and pollution
goals never pass on a city without developed buildings. The goals add to `WinFlags`: a map that
keeps `WinFlags` bit 1 is still won by destroying every enemy; `WinFlags=2` with
`DefeatEnemies=1` requires the goals and the conquest. A deadline is the existing `TimeOut` with
`WinFlags` bit 8 and no `LoseFlags` bit 8 (time out = mission failed); with `LoseFlags` bit 8 the
time-out is a survival victory. `WinFlags` bit 0x10 stays meaningless (protocol 85).

The game evaluates the goals every 32 cycles after the city phase, announces the summary and each
reached goal in the news ticker, and shows an **Objectives** panel at the top right of the map
with each goal's current value, the hold countdown and any time limit. On DuneCity campaign
missions the panel is shown even without city goals: it counts the enemy houses still standing
when destroying them wins, and shows the hold-out or deadline clock. The goals and their hold
state are saved (`SAVEGAMEVERSION` 9887). Protocol 130.

## Timed events: `[EVENTS]`

`BreachN=minute,x,y,width,height[,worms]` opens a mountain wall: at that game minute every
mountain tile in the rectangle becomes sand, the sandworm regions are rebuilt so worms can cross,
up to `worms` (0-8) new Natives sandworms surface in the gap, and the news ticker shows
`BreachNMessage` (default "The Shield Wall has been breached!"). The breach is a saved trigger
(`BreachTrigger`, trigger type 3).

`FirstEnemyAttack=N` is a campaign mission's opening grace period: no QuantBot house hostile to
the objective house launches an attack wave before game minute N (they still build, defend and
fight what comes to them). It is saved with the objectives.

## Briefings: `[BRIEFING]`

`Text=`, `Win=` and `Lose=` are the Mentat's briefing and debriefing for a DuneCity campaign
mission (`BriefingMenu`, with the mission's map file name). Other campaigns keep the TEXT files.

## Campaign rules

`misc/DuneCityCampaign.h`. A game is in the campaign when its scenario file name is
`DCITY_M??.INI`, so saves, retries and the next mission carry it with no other state. Unlike the
original campaign:

- the INI `TechLevel` applies (not the mission number);
- `Brain=TeamN` rivalries are kept (the campaign roster puts every computer house on team 2);
- every QuantBot plays Custom rules: no original-campaign policy (`campaignPolicyApplies`) and no
  Campaign game mode (delayed first attack, army capped at the starting value);
- no Meanwhile or Finale cut-scenes, no region map; online co-op is not offered yet.

## QuantBot and the goals

`[Force Concentration] CityObjectivePlanning` (hashed, default 1; overlay
`tests/ai/play-styles/city-objective-planning-off.ini` for the OFF arm). The QuantBot that
serves the objective house:

- treasury goal: orders Silos until storage plus starting credits can hold the goal, and keeps a
  bank floor of half of all income earned since the start (refined spice plus taxes), so the
  starting cash stays free for the build-up while the treasury rises with the economy; once it
  has added half of what the goal asks beyond the mission's starting treasury (recorded at the
  first evaluation and saved) it spends only the surplus above the goal. A military emergency
  releases the bank. Counting the starting cash as progress froze Tuono's helper from minute 0
  (v3); spending freely until halfway left Brutal and Hard helpers on a few thousand credits for
  45 minutes (v5);
- population goal: its Custom city ceiling is at least 125% of the goal;
- crime or land value goal: police services (first station, catchment placement, land value
  budget) from the first developed block, with police funded at 100% while it has working cash.

```sh
agent-workspace run --project dunecity --owner OWNER --tests --wait -- dunecity-queue submit \
  --purpose research --machines aws aws-mel2 --cwd /Users/stefan/Documents/projects/dunecity-feature-batch \

  --results RESULTS

```

`--categories citycampaign` plays every mission with an idle human and a QuantBot helper one
tier above the enemy QuantBots (Brutal v Hard, Hard v Medium, Medium v Easy), four seeds each by
default (`--seeds-per-cell`, `--city-missions`), 90 game minutes. `win_type` records
`objectives`, `survival` or `conquest`.

## Playtest results

Every round played all ten missions on the AWS workers with the three pairings above; results
live in `/Users/stefan/Documents/projects/outputs/city-campaign-20261119/<round>`.

| Round | What changed | Missions 1-6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | the ten city-scenario layouts, Campaign-mode enemies | 1 and 4 won by objectives in 1-2 min; 2, 3, 5 lost to rushes at 5-13 min | lost | lost at 5 min | lost | probe error (level 10) |
| v3 | grace periods, rebalanced credits, raised goals | 72/72 | 0/8 | 0/12 | 1/9 | 6/12 |
| v5 | Custom rules, harder 1-6, easier 7/9/10, treasury from the starting cash | 72/72 | 6/12 | 6/12 | 3/12 | 12/12 |
| v6 | treasury floor from earned income | 72/72 | 6/7 | 6/12 | 6/12 | 12/12 |

Lessons, each now fixed in the maps or the AI:

- Two Custom-rule enemies that each start richer than the player rush it in the first 5-13 minutes
  even against a Brutal helper; `FirstEnemyAttack` grace periods and player-favoured treasuries
  open the early missions, and both shrink towards mission 10.
- Pre-placed lots develop within a minute, so a prebuilt city's population is effectively its
  starting population: population goals sit well above it (Ash Quarter starts above 45,000).
- A treasury goal needs saving discipline: counting starting cash as progress froze Tuono's helper
  (14,000 of 15,000 at minute 0); spending freely until halfway left Brutal and Hard helpers on a
  few thousand credits; the earned-income floor fills Hagal by minute 11-14.
- Medium helpers beat Easy enemies on every mission in every round: Easy QuantBots barely attack.
  The curve is set by the Brutal v Hard and Hard v Medium pairings.
